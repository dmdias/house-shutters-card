# Changelog

## 1.6.0

- `labels` option: show the names and states over the photo always, only on hover, or never
- `label: hidden` hides the label of one window

## 1.5.1

First public release.

- Roller shutters drawn over a photo of the house, at the position of each cover, with controls (open, stop, close, 50%, slider)
- Lamps in the photo: tap to toggle, warm glow when on
- Swing gates: leaves swinging on their hinges, or a sequence of real photos of the gate opening
- Shutters and lights not visible in the photo as buttons under it
- `house-shutters-grid`: several photos side by side, using the whole width of a panel view
- English and Portuguese
